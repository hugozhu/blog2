## Overview

This privacy policy describes how blog.hugozhu.site collects and uses information when you visit this website.

## Information We Collect

### Analytics

This site uses Google Tag Manager for analytics purposes. This may collect:

- Pages you visit
- Time spent on pages
- Browser type and version
- Device information
- Geographic location (country/city level)

### Comments

This site uses Disqus for comments. When you comment, Disqus collects information according to their own privacy policy. Please review [Disqus Privacy Policy](https://help.disqus.com/en/articles/1717103-disqus-privacy-policy) for details.

### Cookies

This site uses cookies for:

- Analytics tracking (Google Tag Manager)
- Comment functionality (Disqus)

You can disable cookies in your browser settings, though this may affect site functionality.

## Third-Party Services

This site uses the following third-party services:

- **Google Tag Manager** — Analytics and tracking
- **Disqus** — Comment system
- **GitHub Pages** — Hosting

Each service has its own privacy policy governing how they handle data.

## Data Retention

Analytics data is retained according to Google Analytics retention policies. Comment data is retained by Disqus according to their policies.

- Request deletion of your data
- Opt out of analytics tracking
